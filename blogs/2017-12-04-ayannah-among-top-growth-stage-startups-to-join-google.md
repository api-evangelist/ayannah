We are pleased to announce that Google has selected Ayannah to be part of Google's Launchpad Accelerator. The Launchpad Accelerator is a six-month acceleration program that matches top growth-stage startups from emerging ecosystems with the best of Google - its people, network, and advanced technologies - to help scale great products.
