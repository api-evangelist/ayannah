Ayannah and CARD-MRI Group announced a partnership to launch digital payments, lending, banking, and insurance products to serve CARD-MRI’s large and growing membership base. CARD-MRI is the Philippines’ largest microfinance institution with close to seven million members served by over 16,000 associates operating out of over 200 branches spread across the entire archipelago.
